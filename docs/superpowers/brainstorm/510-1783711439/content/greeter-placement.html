Where should the greeter live?

The app today is a left sidebar (the tools "wall") + preview. The greeter is the calm front door. Three ways it can sit against the existing UI — click one.

A · Swap the sidebarREC

The left panel shows the calm greeter by default (size→dates→type→guided/tools). Flipping "Show all tools" swaps that same panel to the full wall. Preview stays put.

B · Full-screen intro

A centered welcome takes over on load (size→dates→type). Pick, and it dismisses into the app — "guided" runs a Q&A, "tools" reveals the sidebar. Most onboarding-like.

C · Stacked in sidebar

Greeter sits at the top of the sidebar; the tools live collapsed right below it. Everything's on one panel — least "calm," most immediate access to the wall.

My lean is A — it matches the spec's "the wall is one toggle away," keeps the preview always visible, and needs no new full-screen layer. But B feels more like a product if you want a true welcome moment.
